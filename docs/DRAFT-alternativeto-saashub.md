## Descrizione (breve)

WhichAI ranks current AI models by their Artificial Analysis score, compares any two side by side, shows what each costs and follows launches and news. Free, with no account and no tracking.

## Descrizione (lunga)

WhichAI is one web page for choosing between AI models. Models are sorted into tiers by the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and plotted on a chart of price against score. You can compare two or three models, see which are free to use through public APIs right now, estimate the monthly cost for your own usage and read the latest launches and price changes. A section with sourced debates covers energy, jobs, copyright and regulation, and a set of tools helps pick a model for a task and write a prompt for it.

The site does not run its own benchmarks. Scores and prices are read automatically from public sources and credited to them. There is no sign-up, no advertising and no tracking; preferences stay in the browser. It works in 11 languages, installs as an app and publishes its data as an open dataset under CC BY 4.0.
