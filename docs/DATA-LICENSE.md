# Licensing: code and data

WhichAI has two halves with two licences.

The source code (everything under `js/`, `api/`, `tools/`, `tests/`, `styles.css`,
`index.html` and the build configuration) is released under the MIT License:
see [`LICENSE.md`](../LICENSE.md). Until v0.39 it was "all rights reserved";
from v0.40 (November 2026) it is open source.

## The data: CC BY 4.0

The model catalog and everything derived from it - `data/models.json`, the
`/api/models`, `/api/benchmarks` and `/api/stats` responses, the scores,
prices, context windows, tags, reviews and source links - are published under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

You may copy, redistribute, remix and build on it, including commercially,
provided you credit: **WhichAI, https://whichai.wiki**.

The underlying benchmark numbers belong to the organisations that produced
them (Artificial Analysis, LMArena, SWE-bench and the vendors). WhichAI
curates, dates and cites them; it does not claim ownership of them.

## The name and brand assets

The MIT License covers the code, not the "WhichAI" name: a fork should use its
own name. The logos under `assets/brands/` belong to their respective owners and
are used to identify the products they refer to. Provenance and terms per file
are documented in `assets/brands/README.md`.
