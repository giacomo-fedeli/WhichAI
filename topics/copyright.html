Copyright and training data

What changes when you train, retrieve or publish with AI? · updated 2026-09-07

Data acquisition, model training, retrieved passages and generated outputs raise distinct questions. Access to a page is not automatically a license to reproduce it. A training dispute also does not determine ownership or infringement for every output you publish.

3 report partsUS Copyright Office studies address replicas, output copyrightability and training; Part 3 is pre-publication
4 factorsUS fair-use analysis considers purpose, nature, amount and market effect together

The concern

Training and outputs can affect creators' licensing and sales markets. Where acquisition was unlawful or outputs substitute for protected expression, 'the model learned patterns' is not a complete answer. Creators need practical ways to discover use and negotiate permissions.

The counter-view

Some uses support research, analysis or other transformative purposes. Individual permission for every input can create costly barriers, especially for smaller developers. The Copyright Office examines varying uses and licensing mechanisms rather than treating all training alike.

The answer depends on jurisdiction, material, acquisition, use and output. This page explains questions to investigate; it does not clear a dataset or publication for legal use.
