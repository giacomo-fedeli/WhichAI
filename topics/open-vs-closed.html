Open weights vs closed models

What control do you gain, and what responsibility comes with it? · updated 2026-09-07

Downloadable weights enable local operation and modification. They do not automatically provide training data, unrestricted permissions, affordable hardware or a secure application. Hosted models trade some control for managed infrastructure; the comparison starts with your requirements.

4 freedomsOSI's definition covers use, study, modification and sharing, with the needed components
2024 reportNTIA recommended monitoring marginal risks and benefits instead of immediate blanket restrictions

The concern

Released weights can be copied and modified beyond the provider's control. Application safeguards matter, but a provider cannot revoke every local copy after finding a problem. Evaluation should examine the extra harmful capability access adds over existing alternatives.

The counter-view

Independent access supports reproducibility, adaptation, competition and use without a model API. These benefits can matter even when a hosted competitor scores higher. Openness aids scrutiny, but the license and released components determine what is possible.

Compare the release, license, hardware and deployment. A closed API can be practical; an open release can be valuable without leading on every task.
