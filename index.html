About WhichAI

How WhichAI differs from a leaderboard

Leaderboards measure models. WhichAI decides what to do with them. It does not run its own benchmarks and does not pretend to: it stands on published evaluations, cites them with a date, and adds the layer none of them cover - going from a goal written in plain language to a model choice and a prompt that model responds well to.

How the data stays current

By itself, every hour, and without a machine ever inventing a number. Figures come from their publishers; words come from people.

  • Scores: official, automatic - the Artificial Analysis Intelligence, Coding and Agentic indices are read from OpenRouter's public model list, which relays the same official numbers published on artificialanalysis.ai. The backend re-reads them every hour; your browser lays them over the catalog on every visit.
  • Tiers and charts recompute themselves - the S to D tiers, the price map, the market figures and the head-to-head defaults are all computed in your browser from the current numbers. Nothing is ranked by hand.
  • New models appear on their own - as soon as Artificial Analysis measures a model it joins the catalog, the ranking and the Radar, marked "auto-added" until a person writes its review. Brand-new models from the big labs show in the Radar even before they are measured.
  • News, read every 30 minutes - headlines from the official OpenAI, Google DeepMind and Mistral AI blogs, TechCrunch AI, Hacker News stories with 100+ points and Hugging Face. Titles and links only: the text belongs to its publishers.
  • Prices and free routes, automatic - prices follow OpenRouter's list and are labelled as such; free routes are the ones that exist right now.
  • Never automatic - reviews, caveats, category ratings and the debates. A model without a current official measurement keeps a converted value marked "~" and is never ranked; a rumored model gets no score at all.

The green "Live data" badge at the top says when the numbers were last read. If the sources are unreachable, the site keeps its last known values and says so.

Methodology
  • Overall score - the official Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, read automatically every hour. Models measured only on an older scale carry a converted value marked "~"; they, and models nobody has measured, are never ranked.
  • Tiers - on the selected index, current models are ranked: S is the top 5%, A the next 15%, B the next 20%, C the next 25%, D the rest; ties share a tier. Legacy models join only when you ask for them.
  • Price map - blended API price (3 parts input to 1 part output, USD per 1M tokens, OpenRouter) on a logarithmic axis. The best-value frontier links the models nothing cheaper beats.
  • Market - "cheapest way into each tier" takes the lowest blended price per tier; "best value" divides the index by the blended price among models at or above the median score; the calculator multiplies OpenRouter prices by the token counts it shows.
  • Category ratings (0-100) - WhichAI blends of public category leaderboards plus editorial judgment. Guidance, not measurements.
  • Task router - per-task rankings with a confidence level (high, medium, low) and linked sources. For general use, analysis and coding the order of Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini follows the live index of each maker's best model; the other tasks are editorial.
  • Independence - no sponsored placements, no affiliate rankings, no accounts, no tracking. Figures correct themselves when their source does; editorial corrections are welcome.

Limits: benchmarks are partial proxies of real work; the live numbers are only as current as their publishers; OpenRouter's price can differ from a vendor's own list; category blends involve judgment.

FAQ
Is WhichAI really free?

Yes, completely. No account, no subscription, no ads, no tracking. It runs on free hosting and needs no key of any kind.

Where do my data go?

Nowhere. Your goals, prompts, preferences and watchlist live only in your browser. WhichAI runs a read-only API that serves the public model catalog and news headlines and nothing else: it accepts no request body, stores nothing and never sees what you type.

Why are generated prompts in English?

Every major model performs measurably best with English instructions. Each generated prompt tells the AI to answer in the language of your task, so you can write your goal in any language and get the answer in your language.

How reliable are the numbers?

The main score is the official Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, read automatically every hour - the same number artificialanalysis.ai publishes. Where no current official number exists we show a clearly marked value ("~") and never rank it; rumored models get no score at all.

Where did output comparison, Chains and Blind Arena go?

Version 0.36 turned WhichAI into one page and removed the tools that needed your own API keys (output comparison, Merge studio, Chains, Blind Arena). Everything left works without any key, account or setup.

Can I contribute?

Yes. The dataset is open under CC BY 4.0 (/api/models, data/models.json), and corrections are the most valuable thing you can send: a wrong score, a missing model, a bad translation, a screen that confused you.

The WhichAI API

Every endpoint is read-only, needs no key, sends CORS headers and is cached at the edge, so anyone can build on the dataset under CC BY 4.0.

  • GET /api/healthStatus, data volume, data dates and the list of endpoints.
  • GET /api/catalogThe self-updating layer: official scores, current prices, free routes and new models, as one small delta.
  • GET /api/modelsThe full live catalog with search, filters, sorting and pagination. Add ?id=claude-opus-5 for one model.
  • GET /api/newsThe Radar news wire: lab blogs, TechCrunch AI, Hacker News and Hugging Face, titles and links.
  • GET /api/benchmarksThe task rankings with sources. Add ?task=coding to run the router server-side.
  • GET /api/recommendSend ?goal= a plain-language goal: the answer is a recommended AI plus a free alternative.
  • GET /api/statsTotals, score distribution, tallies by vendor and tag, leaderboard.
